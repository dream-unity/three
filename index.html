I. The creative territories

Three portals.
Endless possible worlds.

A film can become a world. A composition can give it a pulse. A world can bring people together.

01

The moving image

Film

Give an inner world a visible form. From a first scene to a universe of connected stories, this is the territory of cinema, animation and shared imagination.

The vision for Film +

A place to develop stories, bring collaborators into a production, and share the finished work. Characters, environments and music could carry a story beyond the screen into an explorable world.

Film creation and publishing tools are in development.

02

Beyond the screen

Worlds & VR

Create somewhere to be. Imagine its landscapes, its architecture, its inhabitants and its rules. Build a universe that other people can step inside.

The vision for Worlds +

Connected spaces for exploration, social experiences and collaborative worldbuilding. The ambition is to make creations inhabitable through immersive virtual reality, with ways to encounter them on the web too.

Worldbuilding, traversal and VR experiences are in development.

03

The audible imagination

Music

Give a feeling its own atmosphere. Make songs, scores and soundscapes that stand alone—or become the living sound of a film, a gathering or a universe.

The vision for Music +

A creative home for composing, collaborating and sharing sound. Music can become part of a larger world: a score for its stories, an atmosphere for its places, a reason for its people to gather.

Music creation, collaboration and listening tools are in development.

II. From imagination to encounter

A universe becomes real
when someone else
finds a place in it.

Dream Universe is being built around a simple possibility: what begins in one person’s imagination can become somewhere we meet.

A film opens a door. Music changes the atmosphere. A virtual world gives us a place to explore, create and belong.

III. The social universe

Your imagination.
Our meeting place.

Create with people who see what you see.
Meet people who see what you haven’t.

The vision is a connected creative community: filmmakers finding composers, worldbuilders finding collaborators, and people discovering universes they want to be part of.

Community spaces, creator profiles and collaboration features are in development. This site introduces the vision.
